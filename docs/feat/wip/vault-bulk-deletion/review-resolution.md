# Bulk vault deletion review resolutions

Compared both supplied reviews with the original milestone draft and the vault implementation at `e799ce0`. The 15 submissions identify 13 distinct items. The two overlaps concern preview membership and cached search results. All 13 warrant a design or acceptance-test change. Several resolve choices that the current single-session implementation has never needed to make.

The IDs below follow each review's order. For example, R2.7 is the cached-search finding under Review 2's low-priority heading. Priority reflects the higher of the two ratings for overlapping findings.

Four existing FTS5 tests passed during corroboration: `TestApp_ProjectEditorScopeRefreshAndStaleResults`, `TestApp_RenameRefreshFailureSurfacesError`, `TestMergeCancelledAfterCommittedDeletionBatch` and `TestPrintDeletePreview_WarnsAboutChildren`. They exercise the current single-session, search and merge behavior. The new bulk-deletion acceptance checks are recorded in [tasks.md](tasks.md) for implementation.

The subsequent consistency pass kept all 13 resolutions. It clarified legacy versus batch CLI output, the difference between a stale search reply and a fresh one-time reimport, and Escape/quit handling after confirmation. Task 1 owns store and CLI behavior, Task 4 the interactive deletion preview, and Task 5 its descendant toggle and outside-list labels.
